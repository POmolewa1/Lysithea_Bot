🏆 **WEEKLY STATS & MVP**

Weekly statistics keep track of things such as:

• Games played
• Game time
• Voice activity
• Streaming
• Messages

At the end of each week, an MVP is selected based on their activity.

**MVP Points:**

• 6 points / hour of streaming
• 3 points / hour in voice calls
• 2 points / hour of gaming

(Games that were most popular in the previous week will have a 1/3 point penalty and will be marked with an ❌.)

XP uses a similar point system (does not including any penalties)
